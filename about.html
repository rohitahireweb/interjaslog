OUR STORY

An agile enterprise built on 2 decades of trust

InterJAS is proud to be an agile 21st-century enterprise where trust is inherited and in step with our clients' dynamic business needs. From a single office in Mumbai, we have grown into a multi-modal, multi-branch logistics powerhouse serving India and the world.

Our network spans India's major trade hubs — Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Bangalore, Kolkata, Nagpur, Ahmedabad — and extends globally through a trusted network of partners across Europe, the Americas, the Middle East, and Asia Pacific.
